06 — The Shop

A quiet room.
Sharp results.

Founded in 2020, ADRI & LOKA is a six-chair studio built on one belief: a man’s haircut should work as hard as he does.

No walk-in chaos. No rushed chairs. Espresso on arrival, music low, and a barber who remembers exactly how you like it.

“Look like the man you already are — on your best day.”
